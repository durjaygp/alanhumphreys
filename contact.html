Common Questions

Consultation FAQs

Are video consultations just as effective as in-person?

Yes. Over 60% of Alan's individual consultations are conducted securely via Zoom. High-definition video allows Alan to inspect scalp density, hairline geometry, and evaluate photos effectively.

What should I prepare before my consultation call?

Have a clear idea of your goals, any current hair maintenance routine you use, and photos of your hairline from 3-5 years ago if available. Alan will guide you through the rest.

Does Alan sell high-pressure contracts?

Never. Having bought and built Hair Vital on trust, Alan believes in complete transparency. You will receive honest recommendations without aggressive sales tactics.

How do studio advisory sessions work?

Advisory sessions begin with a comprehensive review of your studio's consultation workflow, retention numbers, and staff training needs, followed by a customized strategy blueprint.
