The 4-Step Maintenance Routine (Developed by Alan)

  1. Step 1: Gentle Release

    Spray Citrus-Clear directly along the front lace edge. Allow 60 seconds for the citrus release agents to penetrate. Lift gently without tugging skin or pulling hairs through the lace base.

  2. Step 2: Scalp Prep & Detox

    Wash your natural scalp with an exfoliating shampoo, then apply Scalp Shield. This creates a micro-invisible layer that blocks perspiration from softening the bond prematurely.

  3. Step 3: Precision Re-Bonding

    Apply two paper-thin layers of Pro-Bond Medical Adhesive to the perimeter, allowing 3 minutes of tack time between layers. Press the system firmly from front hairline to crown.

  4. Step 4: Sun & Hydration Shield

    Finish with 2 mists of UV-Protect Spray. This prevents oxidative color turning (orange/red tones) caused by UV rays and tap water minerals.

💡 Alan's Pro Tip for Active Lifestyles

"If you hit the gym heavily or live in humid climates, double layer the Scalp Shield 10 minutes before applying your adhesive. It adds up to 7 extra days of solid hold during intense workouts."

Will the Citrus Solvent damage fragile French lace?

No. Unlike acetone or harsh industrial solvents, Citrus-Clear breaks down bonds without breaking the polyurethane coating or loosening lace knots.

How long does one kit last?

For standard bi-weekly re-attachments, one kit lasts between 3 to 4 months for individual wearers.

Can I use this kit if I have sensitive scalp or eczema?

Yes, but we always recommend performing a 24-hour patch test behind your ear with the adhesive before a full head installation.
